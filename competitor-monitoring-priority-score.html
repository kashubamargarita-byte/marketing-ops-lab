Frequently asked questions

Can this pull competitor data automatically?
No — this is a manual scoring tool by design, since a static, no-login page can't run background scrapers or store history. Log what you observe (changelog, pricing page, careers page, blog) on your own cadence, then use this to decide how much attention each competitor deserves.
Where do I find this data for each competitor?
Their changelog/release notes page, pricing page (compare screenshots over time), careers page, and blog/resources section are the four highest-signal, easiest-to-check public sources.
How often should I re-run this score?
Re-score every quarter, or immediately after a major competitor event (funding round, leadership change, major launch) — priority can shift quickly.
