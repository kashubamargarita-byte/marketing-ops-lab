Frequently asked questions

What's a good CAC payback period for B2B SaaS?
Under 12 months is a widely used efficiency benchmark for mid-market SaaS; enterprise sales with larger contracts sometimes tolerate 18–24 months given larger deal sizes and expansion revenue.
How is this different from LTV:CAC?
Payback period measures cash efficiency (how fast you get money back); LTV:CAC measures overall profitability of the relationship. A business can have a great LTV:CAC ratio but a slow, cash-straining payback period.
Does expansion revenue change this calculation?
Yes — if customers expand (upsell/cross-sell) after the initial sale, effective payback is faster than this base calculation shows since ARPA typically grows over time.
