Frequently asked questions

How is this different from the Marketing Budget Planner?
The Budget Planner starts from revenue and a fixed % to size a total budget top-down. This tool starts from a specific pipeline/revenue goal and calculates bottom-up exactly what's needed to reach it — useful for sanity-checking whether a top-down budget is actually enough.
What if the budget required looks too high?
That's a signal to improve funnel conversion rates (lead→opp, win rate) or raise average deal size rather than only adding more budget — small conversion improvements compound significantly in this formula.
Should I use blended cost per lead or channel-specific?
Channel-specific if you're planning a specific campaign or channel mix; blended CPL is fine for a rough company-wide sanity check.
