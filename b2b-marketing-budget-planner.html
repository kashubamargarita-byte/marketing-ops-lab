Frequently asked questions

What % of revenue should B2B companies spend on marketing?
Early-stage or high-growth B2B SaaS often spends 10–15%+ of revenue on marketing; established mid-market B2B typically runs 6–9%. Use your growth targets and CAC payback tolerance to pick a number, then stress-test it here.
Should allocation percentages change by growth stage?
Yes — earlier-stage companies typically weight paid and content heavier to build pipeline fast; more mature companies often shift more into ABM, events, and retention/expansion marketing.
Does this include headcount costs?
The 'Team & agencies' bucket is meant to cover people and agency costs — adjust that allocation to match how much of your budget is compensation versus working media.
