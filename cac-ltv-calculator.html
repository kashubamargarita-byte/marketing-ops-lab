Frequently asked questions

Why is 3:1 the benchmark for LTV:CAC?
It's a widely cited rule of thumb (popularized in SaaS/VC circles) implying enough margin to cover acquisition, cost of service, and still be profitable. It's directional, not a law — capital-efficient or early-stage companies sometimes target higher.
Should CAC include only sales & marketing spend, or fully-loaded costs?
Fully-loaded CAC (all sales + marketing spend divided by new customers in the period, including salaries and tools) gives the most honest picture, though many teams start with a simpler paid-spend-only version.
Why use churn to calculate lifetime instead of just asking average tenure?
Churn-based lifetime (1/churn rate) is more reliable early on when you don't yet have years of tenure data to average, and it updates automatically as retention improves or worsens.
