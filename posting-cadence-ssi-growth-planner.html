Frequently asked questions

Is this LinkedIn's official SSI formula?
No — LinkedIn has never published the exact SSI algorithm. This is a directional planning heuristic based on the well-documented pillars (brand, network, insights, relationships) that scales recommended weekly activity to your gap and timeline. Use it for planning, not as a guarantee.
What are the 4 pillars SSI is based on?
Establishing your professional brand, finding the right people, engaging with insights, and building relationships — consistent posting and commenting primarily move the 'brand' and 'insights' pillars.
Can I hit my target faster by just posting more?
Volume alone plateaus — the quality of engagement (comments that spark replies, posts relevant to your ICP) matters more than raw frequency past a baseline cadence.
