Frequently asked questions

What does 'minimum detectable effect' mean?
It's the smallest relative improvement over baseline you want the test to reliably detect. A smaller MDE (e.g. 5% instead of 20%) requires a much larger sample size — you're asking the test to notice a subtler difference.
Why 95% significance and 80% power?
These are the most widely used industry-standard defaults (5% chance of a false positive, 80% chance of detecting a true effect if it exists) — this calculator fixes them for simplicity.
What if I don't have enough traffic to reach this sample size?
Either test a bigger, bolder change (larger expected effect needs a smaller sample), run the test longer, or accept a wider confidence interval and treat results as directional rather than conclusive.
