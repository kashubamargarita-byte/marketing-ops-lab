Frequently asked questions

Why do the two methods give different numbers?
The win-rate method is a pure mathematical inverse of your close rate. The coverage-ratio method is an industry heuristic (commonly 3–4x) that bakes in a safety margin for deal slippage and pipeline that never fully matures — it's usually the higher, more conservative number.
What coverage ratio should I use?
3x is a common B2B SaaS starting benchmark; teams with lower win rates or longer, less predictable cycles often use 4–5x.
Does this account for sales cycle length?
Not directly — this tool sizes pipeline value, not timing. Combine it with your average sales cycle length to figure out how far in advance that pipeline needs to be generated.
