Frequently asked questions

What's a good ROAS for B2B?
It depends entirely on your margins, not a fixed number. A 3x ROAS can lose money if your COGS and delivery costs are high, while 1.5x can be very profitable in a high-margin SaaS business. Always compare your ROAS to your break-even ROAS, not to a generic industry rule of thumb.
How is break-even ROAS calculated?
Break-even ROAS = 1 / (1 − variable cost % of revenue). It's the minimum return needed for revenue to cover ad spend plus the cost of delivering the product or service.
Does this work for lead-gen campaigns, not just e-commerce?
Yes — treat 'revenue' as the value of closed deals attributed to the spend, and 'COGS' as delivery/fulfillment or cost-to-serve for those deals.
