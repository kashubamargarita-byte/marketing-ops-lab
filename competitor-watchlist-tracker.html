Frequently asked questions

Does this actually check competitor sites automatically in the background?
No — and no free, no-login web tool honestly can. Browsers can't silently visit other companies' sites while the tab is closed; that needs a server and scheduled jobs. What this tool automates instead is everything around that: remembering your list, tracking how long it's been, reminding you when it's due, and scoring priority from what you log — so the only manual step left is actually looking at the page.
Where is my competitor list stored?
In your browser's local storage on this device only. It isn't sent anywhere and won't sync across devices — export/re-add if you switch computers.
Where do I find changelog, pricing, and careers URLs for a competitor?
Most B2B SaaS sites have a `/changelog`, `/pricing`, and `/careers` or `/jobs` path. If you can't find a changelog, their release notes are sometimes under `/updates`, `/release-notes`, or a blog tag.
How is the priority score calculated?
The same weighted model as before — release frequency, pricing changes, hiring activity, and your stated strategic overlap — but now pulled automatically from your logged check-ins instead of re-entered by hand each time.
