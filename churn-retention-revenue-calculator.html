Frequently asked questions

Is this gross or net revenue churn?
This is gross customer churn (lost customers × their revenue). It doesn't net against expansion revenue from existing customers — for net revenue retention, subtract expansion/upsell revenue from the loss figure.
What's a healthy monthly churn rate for B2B SaaS?
Under 1–2% monthly (roughly under 10–20% annually) is generally considered healthy for mid-market/enterprise B2B SaaS; SMB-focused products often run higher.
Should this include downgrade, not just full cancellation?
Ideally yes — if you have data on downgrades/contraction, blend it into the churn rate you use here for a more accurate revenue-at-risk figure.
