Frequently asked questions

Should 'conversion' mean a sale or an MQL?
Either — define it consistently for your funnel. For long B2B cycles, use MQL or meeting-booked as the conversion event and an appropriately scaled 'average value' (e.g. pipeline value × win rate) instead of a full deal value.
What's a realistic CTR for B2B email?
B2B click-through rates commonly range 1.5–4% of sends depending on list quality and relevance; segmented, well-targeted lists perform meaningfully better than broad blasts.
Does this account for unsubscribes or list decay?
No — this is a single-campaign snapshot. List health and long-term deliverability should be tracked separately.
