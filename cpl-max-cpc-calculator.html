Frequently asked questions

Why calculate max CPC backward instead of using channel benchmarks?
Benchmarks tell you what others pay on average — not what your specific funnel and deal size can afford. Two companies with the same CPC can have wildly different profitability depending on close rate and deal size.
What counts as 'max acceptable CAC'?
A common B2B rule of thumb is CAC should be no more than 1/3 of first-year contract value, but capital-efficient or bootstrapped teams often target far less. Adjust the % to match your own targets.
Should I use blended or channel-specific conversion rates?
Channel-specific whenever you have enough data — paid social, paid search, and events usually convert differently at every funnel stage.
