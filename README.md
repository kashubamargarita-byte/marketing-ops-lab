# Marketing Ops Lab

**Free, open-source B2B marketing calculators for CMOs, growth teams, and RevOps.** No signup, no paywall, no data collection — every calculation runs client-side in your browser.

🔗 **Live site:** https://kashubamargarita-byte.github.io/marketing-ops-lab

## What's inside

14 interactive calculators across 9 categories, purpose-built for B2B marketing decisions:

| Category | Tools |
|---|---|
| Paid & Budget | ROAS & Break-Even, CPL & Max CPC, B2B Marketing Budget Planner, Campaign Budget vs Pipeline Goal |
| Unit Economics | CAC & LTV, CAC Payback Period |
| Funnel & Conversion | MQL→SQL→Customer Funnel, Sales Cycle & Pipeline Coverage |
| Email & CRM | Email Campaign ROI |
| Content & SEO | Organic Traffic Value |
| Growth & Retention | Churn & Retention Revenue Impact |
| Competitive Intel | Competitor Watchlist Tracker |
| Social | Posting Cadence & SSI Growth Planner |
| CRO & Testing | A/B Test Significance & Sample Size |

## Tech

Plain HTML/CSS/JS, zero build step, zero dependencies, zero tracking. Deployed via GitHub Pages. Every tool page ships with `WebApplication` + `FAQPage` + `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD structured data, unique meta/OG tags, and is explicitly opened to AI/LLM crawlers (GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended) via `robots.txt` and `llms.txt`.
