Frequently asked questions

Which stage should I focus on improving first?
Whichever stage has the lowest conversion rate relative to your benchmarks, or the stage with the highest volume passing through it — improvements there have the largest absolute impact on customers out the bottom.
What are typical benchmark rates for each stage?
They vary hugely by industry and deal size, but common B2B ranges are roughly MQL→SQL 20–50%, SQL→Opportunity 40–60%, Opportunity→Customer (win rate) 15–30%. Use your own historical data whenever you have it.
Should MQL definitions be standardized before using this?
Yes — this model is only as useful as your MQL definition is consistent. Inconsistent lead scoring across time periods will make conversion rates misleading.
